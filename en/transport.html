When to get involved?

The signals showing your transport performance is reaching its limits.

These situations often build up gradually, as your flows multiply across several geographic scales — and become harder to see clearly from outside Europe.

📈 Your transport costs are drifting

Despite one-off renegotiations, margin gains remain limited and temporary.

🌍 You operate at several scales

Domestic, European, international: your flows are managed without a consolidated view.

🤝 Your carrier panel is no longer structured

Providers have multiplied without selection criteria or regular evaluation.

⚠️ Your operational risks are poorly controlled

Safety, regulatory compliance: these topics are still handled case by case.

In most cases, transport performance doesn't decline because of a bad carrier.

It results from management that isn't sufficiently structured across the whole of your transport flows.
My analysis

Four pillars to take control of your transport performance

Every company has its own way of running transport. My role is to understand how to structure, secure and support the management of your operations, whatever their geographic scale.

🤝 Strategy & carrier sourcing

I contribute to benchmarking and structuring your carrier panel at every scale (domestic, European, international), drawing on a network of agents and carriers built over the course of my career. I support the definition of selection criteria, consultations, tenders and rate negotiation.

🛡 Security & transport compliance

I run security and regulatory compliance audits of transport partners, notably as part of AEO (Authorized Economic Operator) certification efforts. I put that same rigor to work securing your transport chain. I also help you define your operational transport standards and track non-conformities and the related action plans.

📊 Performance & management

Before TMS platforms were even common, I designed my own transport management tool: status tracking, costs, door-to-door lead times, carbon footprint, load factor. It let me manage 150 to 200 files a year with the rigor of a professional system. Controlled performance starts with a method, not a piece of software.

⚙️ Continuous improvement & digitalization

I map and standardize your transport processes, identify digitalization opportunities and formalize your business requirements for your tooling projects (TMS in particular) — an approach I have led myself by designing my own management tool.

I don't analyze each topic in isolation.

I study how sourcing, security, performance and digitalization interact across your transport operations, whatever their geographic scale.

Frequently asked questions

What I'm asked most often

Do you act as a freight forwarder?

No.

I work only in advisory and management support. Your company remains the sole principal and direct contracting party with the carriers.

Do you only work internationally?

No.

I work on your domestic, European and international transport operations, depending on your needs.

What's the difference with International Flow Optimization?

Transport focuses specifically on your transport operations, at every geographic scale. International Flow Optimization covers the whole of your international flows (purchasing, Incoterms, customs, Supply Chain), beyond transport alone.

What's the difference with Fractional Supply Chain / Logistics / Transport Leadership?

Transport is a targeted assignment aimed at analyzing a situation, identifying improvement levers and supporting their implementation.

Fractional Supply Chain / Logistics / Transport Leadership consists of supporting the management of your transport function over the long run, a few days a month, by your side.
