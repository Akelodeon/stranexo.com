ABOUT

Why I see international operations differently

I started my career in sales administration, before moving into international transport and import-export, then into management roles as Head of Transport & Import/Export.

That path gave me a global view of international operations, from sales administration all the way to flow management.

My time as a freight forwarder was particularly formative. It's a role that sits at the center of the relationship between companies, suppliers, carriers, customs authorities and every party involved in international operations.

Beyond the analysis, I built these functions from the ground up: an Import team, an Export team and a Transport team, where the work had been scattered across several departments. That ability to structure — not just to recommend — is what I bring to STRANEXO.

You quickly learn that a delay, an extra cost or a difficulty almost never comes from a single issue. A decision made during a purchase, the wrong Incoterm, incomplete documentation, a logistics constraint or a customs requirement can, together, put an otherwise well-prepared operation at risk.

That way of analyzing flows has never left me. It still guides every engagement I take on today, including for US and international companies operating across Europe.

HOW I SUPPORT COMPANIES

A global view, never an isolated topic

I'm convinced that a company doesn't durably improve its performance by optimizing each topic separately.

Transport, Supply Chain, international purchasing, suppliers, Incoterms, customs and procurement are closely connected. A local improvement can sometimes create a problem elsewhere if the whole picture isn't taken into account.

My role is to understand how these decisions interact in order to identify the actions that will have the greatest impact on your organization.

Before proposing solutions, I take the time to listen, analyze and understand how you operate. Because every company is different, every engagement is too.

INDEPENDENT EXPERTISE

STRANEXO is an independent consulting firm

I don't operate transport myself, I don't file customs declarations, and I don't receive any commission from carriers, freight forwarders or other providers I may recommend to you.

That independence lets me advise you with full objectivity, with a single goal: identifying the solutions best suited to your company.

My role is to bring you expertise, an outside perspective and objective recommendations. The decisions remain yours.

WHY I CREATED STRANEXO

A simple conviction

After several years spent managing international operations inside a company, I chose to create STRANEXO around a simple conviction: SMEs and mid-market companies — including US and international companies operating in Europe — should be able to access independent strategic expertise, not just the advice of their transport partners, without having to hire a full-time leader on the ground.

I support leadership teams who want to structure their Supply Chain, improve the performance of their international flows and secure their operations.

The goal isn't to apply a standardized method, but to build solutions suited to your organization, your challenges and your growth ambitions.
