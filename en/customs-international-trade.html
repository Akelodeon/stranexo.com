CUSTOMS & INTERNATIONAL TRADE

Secure your European operations before risk turns into cost.

Customs & International Trade for US and international companies operating in Europe

Delays, extra costs and customs disputes usually take root long before the declaration is even filed.

An Incoterm choice, an international supplier, a tariff classification, incomplete documentation, or a regulatory change that wasn't anticipated in time: taken individually, these decisions look harmless. Added together, they can put your European operations at risk — especially when they're managed from outside the region.

I help US and international companies identify and master these risks in order to durably secure their European operations.

WHEN DOES THIS EXPERTISE BECOME RELEVANT?

This expertise matters most when your international activity is moving faster than your practices — and your team on the ground.

🌍 New markets

You're entering new markets or growing your import or export volumes in Europe.

🤝 Internal coordination

Several teams — sometimes across two continents — work on your international operations without always sharing the same view.

❓ Unclear responsibilities

Responsibilities between your suppliers, carriers and customers lack clarity.

⏱️ Delays and holdups

You're seeing delays, holdups or costs that are hard to explain.

🛡️ Preparing for an audit

You want to check the robustness of your organization before a customs audit or a dispute arises.

In many companies, difficulties don't come from a single mistake. They appear when several decisions made across different teams — sometimes across different time zones — are no longer sufficiently coordinated.
WHAT I ANALYZE

My role is to understand how decisions made in purchasing, logistics, Supply Chain, sales or administration interact with customs requirements.

🛃 Classification and value

Tariff classification of goods, origin and customs value.

📄 Incoterms and documentation

Incoterms, allocation of responsibilities with your carriers, and customs procedures.

🌐 Overall organization

Your suppliers' practices and the organization of your international flows.

The goal isn't to check boxes on a compliance form. It's to identify the situations likely to generate operational, financial or regulatory risk.

MY APPROACH

A pragmatic approach, grounded in your operational reality.

I don't apply a standardized method. I analyze your organization as a whole to identify the decisions that could have customs, regulatory or operational consequences.

Based on that analysis, I build a realistic, prioritized action plan with your teams, tailored to your objectives.

I do not act as a licensed customs broker.

My role is to give you an overall view, secure your decisions and support your teams in implementing the improvements you choose.
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to the main questions about Customs & International Trade.

Do you file customs declarations?

No. I work as a consultant. Declarative formalities are handled by licensed operators or by your specialized partners.

Is this expertise limited to customs?

No. It also covers Incoterms, contractual responsibilities, documentary organization, supplier practices and, more broadly, the decisions that affect the compliance and security of your operations in Europe.

What's the difference with International Flow Optimization?

International Flow Optimization aims to improve the overall performance of your flows. This expertise aims to master the customs and regulatory risks that could compromise that performance.

Is this expertise suited to US and international companies?

Yes. It's built specifically for US and international companies that want to durably structure their European operations and secure their growth, without a full-time presence on the ground.
