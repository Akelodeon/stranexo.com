Client case

Initial situation: A long-standing pallet freight carrier had been raising its rates every year without pushback, to the point of a significant gap with actual market conditions. When the account was picked up, a new 4% increase had just been announced.

Diagnostic: Analysis of existing rate grids, benchmark against current market conditions, and identification of renegotiation room across two transport partners.

Actions: Renegotiation bringing the long-standing carrier's rates back in line with the market, cancelling the announced increase and securing a 15% reduction on the existing offer. On the second partner (parcel freight), renegotiation of the applied rate structure.

Results: $60K in recovered margin over 12 months on the first lever, and 40% in additional margin captured over 12 months on the second.
