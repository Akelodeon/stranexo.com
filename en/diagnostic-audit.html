Scope of intervention

A fact-based diagnostic, built from your real data

Depending on the package chosen, the analysis covers in particular:

  • the organization of your logistics and Supply Chain function;
  • your transport costs and performance;
  • your purchasing processes and your Incoterms;
  • your tariff classification, origins and customs procedures;
  • your exposure to customs reassessment risk;
  • your existing management indicators.

Every diagnostic is built on your real data (invoices, flows, procedures) and on interviews with your teams.

My approach

A fast, fact-based method, without jargon

A diagnostic or an audit only has value if it stays usable. I favor clear deliverables, prioritized by impact and directly understandable by your leadership team.

The typical process includes an initial scoping of your challenges, an analysis of your data and your practices on the ground, followed by a debrief with a prioritized action plan.

You remain entirely free to decide what happens next: internal implementation, targeted support on one specific lever, or moving to a broader International Flow Optimization engagement.

Frequently asked questions

What I'm asked most often

What's the difference between the Flash Diagnostic and the full Audit?

The Logistics Flash Diagnostic is a first, fast assessment, designed to identify your main levers quickly. The International Logistics Performance Audit goes further: it analyzes your flows, your costs and your organization in depth.

Does the customs audit replace an audit by a licensed customs broker?

No.

My role is to identify your areas of risk and your levers for securing them, with full independence. Depending on what's found, I can point you toward the relevant regulatory experts for specific issues.

Am I committed to implementing the recommendations?

No.

The diagnostic or audit is yours. You remain entirely free to decide what to do with it, internally or with additional support.

What's the difference with International Flow Optimization?

Diagnostic & Audit is a targeted first step that validates a specific situation. International Flow Optimization is a broader engagement that also supports the implementation of the levers identified.
